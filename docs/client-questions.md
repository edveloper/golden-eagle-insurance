## Google Business Profile: hide the address

Do this signed in to the Google account that manages the profile.

1. Search "Golden Eagle Insurance Agency" on Google. The owner's controls appear above the results. (Or open business.google.com.)
2. Choose **Edit profile**, then **Location**.
3. Under **Business location**, turn off "Show business address to customers", or remove the address. Save.
4. Under **Service area**, add the areas they serve, for example Nairobi and Kiambu. Save.
5. Google may ask to re-verify. Reviews and the profile link stay the same.
