What changes regardless

These fixes apply whichever direction you choose. They come from the audit.

Readable goldGold text on white is 2.5:1 today. A darker text gold (#7a5c10, 5.7:1) for words, and the bright gold kept for buttons and lines.
Three hero sizesToday all 11 pages share one hero. Big photo heroes only on Home and Advisory, medium on About and Insurance, and a plain text header on utility and legal pages.
Break the section templateLeft-aligned headings by default, with centred ones only for closing calls to action. Fewer gold eyebrow labels, fewer boxed cards, no icons in tinted squares.
One paletteRemove the stray blue. Navy, gold and one considered neutral in place of flat grey #f5f5f5.
Real photosNon-AI photographs of Kenyan people and places, licence-checked, with every source recorded in a credits file.
Header and footerThe header slims down on scroll, the mobile menu gets a proper Call / WhatsApp / Quote bar, and the footer is tidied up.

My recommendation: A for the main site, with C used only on the Advisory page and the homepage's advisory band. That gives the investment arm the boutique sub-brand the client asked for, while the insurance side stays calm and readable.
