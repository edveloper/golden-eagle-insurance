Choose the Wordmark

The eagle stays as it is. Only the name beside it changes. Capitals with wide spacing give the name the presence the plain version lacks. Everything in this kit uses the recommended version.

Logo

Use navy on light backgrounds and white with gold on navy. Use the stacked version for square spaces, and the eagle on its own where the name won't fit or is already nearby.

The eagle only exists as a PNG today. For print (signage, letterheads, merchandise), ask whoever designed it for the original vector file (.ai, .eps, .svg or .pdf).

Colour

Navy leads, gold accents. Gold has two versions: a bright gold for buttons, lines and the eagle, and a darker gold for small text on light backgrounds, where the bright gold is too faint to read.

Using the Logo

  • Clear space: keep empty space around the logo at least as tall as the eagle’s head.
  • Minimum size: the horizontal logo at 140px wide on screen (35mm in print); the eagle on its own at 24px.
  • Photos: place the logo only on photos darkened with navy, as in the covers above.
Don’t stretch or squash the eagle. Resize it with its proportions locked.
Don’t recolour it outside navy, gold and white.
Don’t add shadows, outlines or gradients.
Don’t set the name in another typeface.
